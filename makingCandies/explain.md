This function calculates the minimum passes needed to produce n candies starting with m machines and w workers. Each pass produces m * w candies; excess candies can buy machines or workers at cost p each. The algorithm simulates passes, tracking the minimum passes needed by calculating when the target could be reached with current production. If candies fall short of the purchase cost, it fast-forwards passes. After each purchase, it balances machines and workers by keeping the larger count at least half the total, maximizing production efficiency. Returns the minimum passes among all calculations.
